What the marks mean

Red. This line cannot be used yet. Something you need to price it (the product, the INN, the unit price or the currency) could not be read from the document, or was not in it. Open it, look at the source, then reject or hold it.

Amber. The values are there, but one of them was reached in a way that deserves a human look. The usual reasons: the unit price was calculated from the pack price; the document corrected a value further down; the price depends on quantity and the lowest tier is shown; a value does not appear word for word in the document, so it could not be confirmed automatically.

No mark. Every key value appears word for word in the document and passed the checks. Approve without opening, or spot-check.

Correcting a value

Open a line and click any value to type what the document says. A corrected value shows "set by reviewer" with the original beside it, clears that field's mark, and goes with the decisions when you export. approve applies corrections before decisions and keeps both values in the audit.

Decisions

Approve sends the line on, Hold keeps it for later, Reject drops it. Decisions stay in this browser until you press the button at the top: Apply decisions when the page is served by docintel review, which applies them and writes approved.csv straight away; Save decisions when the page was opened from disk, which saves decisions.json for docintel approve.

Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to toggle. Esc closes.
